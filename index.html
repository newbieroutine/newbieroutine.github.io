YouTube Transcript & Subtitle Exporter

A Chrome extension that saves the transcript of a YouTube video as a text file, or copies it to your clipboard. Guide: copy a YouTube transcript into NotebookLM or ChatGPT.

Get it on the Chrome Web Store

What it does

  • Exports the captions of the video you are watching as TXT, SRT, VTT or Markdown.
  • Copies the text to your clipboard, or copies it with your prompt and opens ChatGPT or Claude, so you can paste it there.
  • Joins broken auto-generated caption lines into readable paragraphs. Timestamps are optional.
  • Removes sound tags like [Music] and [Applause] from text exports, if you want.
  • Lets you pick any caption track the video has. Optional translation uses the translator built into Chrome.
  • Works on regular videos and Shorts.

It exports caption text only. It does not download video or audio.

It only works on videos that already have captions (uploaded or auto-generated). There is no speech-to-text.

Your data stays in your browser

The captions are fetched from the YouTube page and turned into a file on your computer. They are not sent to me or to any server. No account, no ads, no analytics. The optional Pro AI transcription is different: it sends short audio clips of the video you are playing to our server to turn speech into text, and stores neither the audio nor the text. Details are in the Privacy Policy and the extension's privacy policy.

Price

Exporting a single video is free, with no limit, and will stay free. Pro ($4.99, one-time) adds AI transcription for videos that have no captions. See Pricing.
