Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

For the Chrome extension itself, the full privacy policy is here:
YouTube Transcript & Subtitle Exporter: Privacy Policy.

In short: transcripts are processed in your browser and are not sent to me. Data reaches me only when you choose to send it, for example a problem report.

This page covers the Corn Tools website and buying Pro.

1. This website

  • No analytics, no tracking scripts, no ads.
  • No cookies.
  • No forms. Nothing you type here is collected.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security and operations. GitHub's privacy statement applies to that.

2. Buying Pro (once available)

  • Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, our Merchant of Record. Your card or other payment details go to Dodo Payments and its payment partners. I never see or store them. Dodo Payments' own privacy policy applies to that data.
  • From Dodo Payments I receive only what is needed to provide and support your purchase: your email address, your name if you entered one, your country (for tax), the order ID, amount and date, and the status of your license key.
  • When you activate Pro, the extension sends your license key to Dodo Payments to check that it is valid. No transcripts or browsing data are sent.

3. How I use it

To deliver your license, answer your support emails, keep tax and accounting records, and prevent abuse of license keys. I don't sell your data, and I don't send marketing emails unless you asked for them.

4. How long I keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed for your license to work and as required by tax and accounting rules. Support emails are kept while they are useful for helping you.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing 603356553@qq.com. Some order records may need to be kept for legal reasons.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
